## Description

```
In September 1859 the Sun hit the Earth with the biggest storm ever recorded. Gold miners got up at 1 a.m. thinking it was morning, auroras lit up Cuba and Hawaii, and telegraph wires threw sparks and set paper on fire. In July 2012 the Sun fired another storm just as big — and missed us by about a week. This is what a direct hit would do to a world that now runs on electricity, and how we'd see the next one coming.
```
