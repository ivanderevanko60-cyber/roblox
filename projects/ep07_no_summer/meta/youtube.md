In June 1816 it snowed in New England. In Europe it rained for months, the harvest rotted and people starved — and in a villa on Lake Geneva, an eighteen-year-old stuck indoors by the storms started writing Frankenstein. The cause was a volcano on the other side of the world that had erupted a year earlier: Tambora, the biggest eruption in recorded history.
