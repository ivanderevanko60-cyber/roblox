## Description

```
Sunlight takes about eight minutes to reach us — but the number behind that has no error bar at all. The speed of light is exactly 299,792,458 metres per second, because in 1983 we stopped measuring it. This is the 300-year story of how we got there: a late moon of Jupiter, a wheel with 720 teeth, two mountains in California and a laser.
```
