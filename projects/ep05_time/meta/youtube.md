On 30 June 2012 the world added one second to the clock — 23:59:60 — and Reddit, airline check-in systems and servers across the internet fell over. That second exists because the Earth is a bad clock, and because right now nobody on Earth knows exactly what time it is: official time is calculated weeks later, near Paris, by averaging hundreds of atomic clocks.
