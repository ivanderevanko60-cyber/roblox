## Description

```
There is no rock on Earth as old as the Earth — so how do we know the planet is 4.54 billion years old? The answer came from a meteorite, and from a chemist who could not measure it because everything around him was contaminated with lead. To date the Earth, Clair Patterson built one of the first clean rooms in science. To explain the lead, he took on the petrol industry.
```
